        Part of the link probably got cut off when it was copied. Ask whoever sent it for a fresh one, or start your own night out.

        Votes live in the link and on your phone; there's no account and no server. Bar data comes from OpenStreetMap contributors, looked up through the Overpass API or, when that's busy, OSM's Nominatim; they see your approximate location only when you search. The map comes from OpenFreeMap, which sees the area you're looking at. Visits are counted with cookie-free Plausible.
